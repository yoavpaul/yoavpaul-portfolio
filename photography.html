Monsters Under The Bed - Guy Jarzin

Four noble truths before bedtime 2023

A project exploring the concept of limitation — recognizing and accepting the events in our lives that lie beyond our control.

In the quiet hours before sleep, thoughts and emotions emerge without the distractions of everyday life. Inspired by Pema Chödrön’s When Things Fall Apart, the project reflects on our reactions to uncertainty and the possibility of transforming resistance into acceptance.

“What might appear to be an arrow or a sword, we can actually experience as a flower.”

The work was exhibited at Beit Herzl in collaboration with an artist collective, alongside a chef’s dinner inspired by the theme of limitation.

Materials:
Analog photography, 120 and 35mm film

Special thanks:
Eden Amit, Nir Erez, Tami and Ami from Panda
