I’M HERE?

I’m Here? — Lino Prints Series

A series of three lino prints originally created for the entrance exams to Weißensee Academy of Art Berlin, later exhibited at Bezalel as part of the “I Am Here” exhibition, Inspired by the work of Israeli designer David Tartakover.
The prints reflect the Israeli reality of the past three years and invite viewers to connect from both Israeli and Palestinian perspectives, using familiar symbols and colors that speak to shared cultural identities.
